# MITRE ATT&CK attribution

This project uses MITRE ATT&CK technique identifiers, tactic/technique names, and lookup metadata. ATT&CK is a registered trademark of The MITRE Corporation. Use does not imply MITRE endorsement.

- [MITRE ATT&CK terms of use](https://attack.mitre.org/resources/terms-of-use/)
- [MITRE ATT&CK data repository](https://github.com/mitre-attack/attack-stix-data)
- [Imported lookup source and hashes](../pkg/PROVENANCE.md)

The lookup source describes its Enterprise ATT&CK data as v16. It is not a live or complete mirror of the current ATT&CK knowledge base. This source preparation preserves the existing lookup rather than downloading or asserting an unverified upstream STIX revision. A future data refresh must record its exact upstream revision, transformation, license/notice files, and resulting hashes.

The separate historical TRAM attribution is preserved at [TRAM-NOTICE.txt](../internal/eval/testdata/TRAM-NOTICE.txt). The new synthetic sentences are not quotations from ATT&CK reports or the TRAM corpus.
